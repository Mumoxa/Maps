## Scoring model (100 pts) — applied per candidate in report
A Scope match (20): month-end, GL, AFS, VAT, AR/AP, payroll accounting, budget, tax, audit support — breadth and recency.
B Industry (15): 15=construction materials/aggregates/industrial mfg direct; 10–12=industrial mfg/mining/energy/freight; 8–9=large multi-site retail/distribution w/ group reporting; 5–7=fintech/media/insure/edu/tech; ≤4=unrelated.
C Role vs scale (15): current role level vs SATURC senior-accountant band, scale-adjusted (FM at SME ≈ target; Controller/Group Acct at MM ≈ target; FM/GM at VL = one level high, capped).
D Qualifications (15): BCom Accounting/equiv + confirmed SAICA articles (15); CA(SA) recent (10–12 PQE, exception-flagged if senior); PA(SA)/AGA(SA) (8–12); degree-only, articles unconfirmed (6–8); weaker (≤5).
E Multi-entity/group (10): confirmed group reporting/consolidation (9–10); strong inference (7–8); none (0–5).
F Industrial accounting (10): cost/production/stock/FA/quarrying/plant evidence (8–10); general mfg (6–7); none (0–4).
G Leadership (5): confirmed team lead (4–5); inferred (2–3); none (0).
H ERP (5): confirmed named system (4–5); "ERP experience" generic (2); none (0–1).
I Geography (5): Zone A (0–15 min: Durbanville, Brackenfell, Parow, Bellville, Parow, Industria/Elsies R edge, Maitland) = 5; Zone B (15–35 min: Cape Town North/South Suburbs, Goodwood, Strand edge) = 4; Zone C (>35 min: Stellenbosch, Worcester, Malmesbury, Caledon, Simon's Town, JHB/other province) = 2–3.
