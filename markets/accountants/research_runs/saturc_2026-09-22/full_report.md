## 2. Method (company-first, capability-first)

1. **Employer universe built first** (01_employer_universe.md): Tier 1 = construction materials/aggregates/quarrying/industrial mfg with WC operations (Afrimat, Portland Group, AfriSam, UltraTech/PPC, Leonis, Megamix, Ciolli, Coastal, TimberCity/TBC, Koeberg/Eskom, Oceana, Transnet, DP World, Renesys, K92, EPC firms, Bass, Mzansi, Econolease); Tier 2 = large multi-entity employers with group-finance functions (Pepkor, Shoprite, Media24, Vuka, Penbev, Weylandts, Clicks, Ten, TFG, Old Mutual, Clicks, Curro, PostNet, Armscor, Sanlam, Capitec, Clicks, De Beers, Philip Morris, Southern Sun, Globeleq, Lewis, Fourways, Corruseal, SGHC); Tier 3 = adjacent (retail, food, logistics, agri).
2. **Discovery engines (in yield order):**
   (a) LinkedIn public "similar profiles" widgets on seed profiles (9 batches);
   (b) **ZoomInfo title directories for Cape Town** (9 title pages: senior accountant, financial accountant, group accountant, group reporting accountant, financial controller, finance manager, management accountant, cost accountant) — 9 names/page with company;
   (c) **RocketReach/ZoomInfo company finance-department pages** (Afrimat, Penbev, Pepkor Speciality, Media24, Oceana, Vuka, Energy Group, Montagu, Weylandts, Coastal, Anderson Lid);
   (d) job-board corroboration of employer finance functions (Portland Group, Vuka, TBC, West Coast Personnel, Montagu);
   (e) per-name aggregator verification (ZoomInfo/RocketReach/SignalHire/ContactOut/LinkedIn).
3. **Every fact below traces to a retrieved source URL.** Confidence labels: **HIGH** = ≥2 independent sources or full LinkedIn profile; **MEDIUM** = one solid source (aggregator page with work history) + no contradicting source; **LOW** = single thin source (directory row, widget row) — these carry an explicit screening item.
4. **Scoring:** 100-point model A–I (weights in 04_scoring_notes.md). Fit Score and Evidence Confidence are separate columns. No title-first ranking: level was always judged *role-vs-scale* (an FM at a 50-person manufacturer scored at target level; a Group Manager at a 10,000-person group scored one level high).
5. **No protected characteristics** were used in search, scoring, or ranking.
6. **Exclusions** are logged with reasons (04_scoring_notes.md): wrong location (incl. 3 "moved overseas/JHB" false positives from stale data), pure banking/insurance/funds, practice-only with no commerce move, C-suite, pure treasury/tax/internal-audit, and juniors.
