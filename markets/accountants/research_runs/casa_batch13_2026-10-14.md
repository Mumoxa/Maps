## Evidence rules applied

- Designation: only explicit `CA(SA)` / “Chartered Accountant (SA)” wording on
  a SAICA, employer, company-disclosure or CFO South Africa source. “Eligible
  CGMA” (Sibabalwe Bebeza) was **not** counted as CGMA. CFO award ≠ CA(SA)
  (Kevin Jacoby, Mikaeel Tayob).
- Currency: roles corroborated on a live employer page or a 2025/2026 source
  are `CONFIRMED`; roles stated only on a 2023/2024 award profile are
  `HIGH_CONFIDENCE` with a HISTORICAL-ROLE FLAG in `notes` — dated job
  information is not presented as re-verified current employment.
- Location: kept separate from designation/employment. Person-specific office
  or residence/workplace statements = `CONFIRMED` (Sanele Nkosi — BDO Durban;
  Sibabalwe Bebeza — BDO Johannesburg; Dorcas Nhlapho — BDO Johannesburg
  office; Christiaan Barnard — Cape Town residence + CBD commute).
  Employer-based location = `HIGH` (Brecht Mohonathan, Sandiso Gcwabe).
  A South African employer, a “South Africa” country default, nationality,
  origin/hometown and directory/LinkedIn location snippets were **not** used
  as location proof (17 of the 23 new records therefore have no SA location
  evidence yet).
- Industry: the sourced **current employer's sector** (e.g. BBF Safety Group
  = Manufacturing per SAICA's “largest safety footwear manufacturer”), never
  a personal specialism and never “Accounting” merely for being a CA(SA).
  All 23 new records carry a sourced employer sector; sector provenance for
  the whole inventory is reported as PERSON_RECORD vs EMPLOYER_CATALOGUE in
  `ca_sa_inventory.csv`.
- LinkedIn: left blank for 22 of 23 new people. The single exception
  (Carel Snyman) is hyperlinked from his employer's own leadership page —
  no URL was constructed from a name or taken from a search-result slug.
  Omar Ahmed Baker's search-result LinkedIn URL was deliberately not recorded.
- Skills, systems and training routes: nothing inferred from a designation.
  Articles are recorded only where a source explicitly states completion at
  a named office (Mike Davis — Deloitte; Christiaan Barnard — PKF Inc.).
- No gated directory or LinkedIn scraping; no contact details recorded.
  Directory sites (ZoomInfo/SignalHire-type) were used, if at all, only as
  discovery/conflict flags and are not cited as evidence in person records.
