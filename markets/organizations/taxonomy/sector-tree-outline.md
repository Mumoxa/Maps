- **Mining, Quarrying & Extractive Industries** `MIN` — SIC 05-09
  - Bushveld Complex PGM and chrome belt, Witwatersrand and Free State gold, Mpumalanga and Waterberg coal, Northern Cape iron ore and manganese, Kimberley and West Coast marine diamonds. Junior miners and black-owned small-scale operators sit in the same tree as the JSE-listed majors.
  - Also known as: Mining, Extractives, Mining houses, Mineral resources
  - **Platinum Group Metals & Chrome** `MIN-PGM`
    - Rustenburg, Marikana, Northam, Steelpoort and Mokopane. Post-demerger the listed PGM major trades as Valterra Platinum (JSE: VAL, formerly Anglo American Platinum).
    - Also known as: PGM, Platinum, Bushveld Complex, Chrome
    - **PGM Mine Operations** `MIN-PGM-MINE`
      - Underground and open-pit PGM extraction, shaft and decline development, hoisting and winding.
      - **Deep-Level Mechanised Stoping** `MIN-PGM-MINE-DEEP`
        - Merensky reef mechanised stoping, trackless mobile machinery fleets, seismic management.
      - **Platreef Bulk Mining** `MIN-PGM-MINE-PLATREEF`
        - Mokopane / Platreef bulk mechanised operations and associated smelter feed contracts.
      - **UG2 Reef Operations** `MIN-PGM-MINE-UG2`
        - Chromitite-hosted UG2 extraction, typically chrome credit driven.
    - **PGM Concentration, Smelting & Refining** `MIN-PGM-PROC`
      - Concentrators, furnace and converter operations, base metals refineries and auto-catalyst precursor supply chains.
      - **Base Metals Refining** `MIN-PGM-PROC-BM`
      - **Auto-Catalyst Precursor Supply** `MIN-PGM-PROC-CAT`
        - Precious metal chemical supply into catalytic converter manufacturers, including component plants in the Eastern Cape and Gauteng automotive clusters.
    - **Chrome Concentrate Production** `MIN-PGM-CHROME`
      - Chrome extraction and concentrate for the domestic ferrochrome industry, largely supplying Nkangala and North West smelters.
    - **Junior PGM Exploration & Development** `MIN-PGM-JR` _(informal economy)_
      - The SMME end of PGM: prospecting right holders, junior developers and black-owned mining companies under MPRDA small-scale and artisanal provisions.
      - **Greenfield & Brownfield Exploration** `MIN-PGM-JR-EXPL`
      - **Black-Owned Junior & Small-Scale Mining** `MIN-PGM-JR-BLACK`
        - MPRDA small-scale mining permits, junior B-BBEE Level 1-2 operators and community-held mineral rights.
  - **Gold** `MIN-GOLD`
    - Witwatersrand basin, Free State goldfields and the Mpumalanga and Barberton greenstone belts. Deep-level cost pressure has pushed the sector into tailings retreatment, mechanised mining and closure economics.
    - **Deep-Level Underground Gold Mining** `MIN-GOLD-DEEP`
      - **Shaft Sinking & Level Development** `MIN-GOLD-DEEP-SHAFT`
    - **Tailings Retreatment & Surface Dumps** `MIN-GOLD-TAIL`
      - Slimes dam and dump retreatment, re-processing of historic tailings, uranium by-product handling.
    - **Gold Refining, Assaying & Minting** `MIN-GOLD-REF`
      - Rand Refinery-type refining, London Good Delivery bar production, Krugerrand minting.
    - **Junior Gold Developers** `MIN-GOLD-JR`
    - **Artisanal & Informal Gold Extraction** `MIN-GOLD-ART` _(informal economy)_
      - Zama zama operations in disused and working shafts. Recorded in the taxonomy because it is a real part of the registered and unregistered economy, not because it is legal: licensing status is captured separately under regulatory vectors.
      - **Informal Aggregation & Gold Buying** `MIN-GOLD-ART-AGG`
  - **Coal** `MIN-COAL`
    - Mpumalanga Highveld, Waterberg and Vryheid fields, split between export-grade and Eskom-supply contracts, with a long tail of small collieries supplying local industry.
    - Also known as: Coal mining, Collieries
    - **Export-Grade Thermal & Coking Coal** `MIN-COAL-EXP`
      - Richards Bay Coal Terminal export chain, RB1/RB3 grades.
    - **Eskom-Supply Domestic Coal** `MIN-COAL-ESC`
      - Tied collieries and short-term supply contracts feeding Mpumalanga power stations.
    - **Coal Washing & Beneficiation** `MIN-COAL-WASH`
    - **Small-Scale Collieries** `MIN-COAL-JR` _(informal economy)_
      - Emerging black-owned collieries and contract mining permits supplying domestic industry.
    - **Coal Hauling & Stockyard Logistics** `MIN-COAL-HAUL`
      - Side-tipper road haulage and stockyard management; cross-referenced to TRA-ROAD-BULK.
  - **Iron Ore, Manganese & Bulk Minerals** `MIN-IRON`
    - Northern Cape Kalahari manganese and iron ore fields (Sishen, Kolomela), rail-linked to Saldanha and Port Elizabeth.
    - **Iron Ore Mining Operations** `MIN-IRON-ORE`
    - **Manganese Mining Operations** `MIN-IRON-MANG`
    - **Beneficiation & Sinter Feed** `MIN-IRON-BEN`
    - **Heavy-Haul Rail & Port Interface** `MIN-IRON-RAIL`
      - Ore line slot allocation and port stockpile management; cross-referenced to TRA-RAIL.
  - **Diamonds** `MIN-DIAM`
    - Kimberley and Cullinan kimberlites, Namaqualand and West Coast alluvial and marine operations, plus the cutting and polishing beneficiation layer.
    - **Kimberlite Mining Operations** `MIN-DIAM-KIMB`
    - **Alluvial & Marine Diamond Mining** `MIN-DIAM-ALLU`
    - **Sorting, Valuation & Rough Trading** `MIN-DIAM-SORT`
    - **Diamond Cutting & Polishing** `MIN-DIAM-CUT`
      - dtic-designated beneficiation activity concentrated around Johannesburg.
    - **Artisanal Alluvial Digging** `MIN-DIAM-ART` _(informal economy)_
  - **Battery & Critical Minerals** `MIN-BAT`
    - Vanadium, lithium pegmatites, copper, nickel, zinc, titanium and rare earths, plus mid-stream precursor processing driven by the just energy transition.
    - **Vanadium & Titanium** `MIN-BAT-VAN`
    - **Lithium & Pegmatite Minerals** `MIN-BAT-LI`
    - **Copper, Nickel & Zinc** `MIN-BAT-CU`
    - **Rare Earth Elements** `MIN-BAT-REE`
    - **Mid-Stream Processing & Precursor Plants** `MIN-BAT-PROC`
  - **Quarrying, Aggregate & Industrial Minerals** `MIN-QUARRY` _(informal economy)_
    - The largest count of registered micro and small extractive entities in the country: aggregate quarries, sand pits and clay works supplying local construction.
    - **Crushed Stone & Aggregate** `MIN-QUARRY-AGG`
    - **Building & River Sand** `MIN-QUARRY-SAND`
    - **Dimension Stone & Granite** `MIN-QUARRY-DIM`
    - **Limestone & Dolomite** `MIN-QUARRY-LIME`
      - Agricultural lime, steel flux and cement feedstock.
    - **Clay, Kaolin & Industrial Minerals** `MIN-QUARRY-CLAY`
  - **Mining Services & Mine Support** `MIN-SV`
    - Contract mining, drilling, shaft sinking, equipment supply and safety services. This is where most SMME participation in mining actually happens.
    - **Contract & Shaft Mining** `MIN-SV-CONTRACT`
      - **Contract Shaft & Decline Mining** `MIN-SV-CONTRACT-SHAFT`
      - **Small-Scale Contract Mining Crews** `MIN-SV-CONTRACT-SMME` _(informal economy)_
    - **Shaft Sinking & Raise Boring** `MIN-SV-SHAFT`
    - **Drilling & Blasting** `MIN-SV-DRILL`
    - **Mining Equipment Supply & Rebuild** `MIN-SV-EQ`
    - **Mine Health, Safety & PPE Supply** `MIN-SV-SAFE`
    - **Mine Labour Provision & Training** `MIN-SV-LAB`
  - **Mine Closure, Rehabilitation & Water** `MIN-REHAB`
    - Closure planning, revegetation and acid mine drainage treatment - a growing SMME services market as legacy mines close.
    - **Mine Closure & Decommissioning** `MIN-REHAB-CLOS`
    - **Revegetation & Soil Rehabilitation** `MIN-REHAB-REVEG`
    - **Acid Mine Drainage & Water Treatment** `MIN-REHAB-WATER`
- **Energy, Power & Green Transition** `ENR` — SIC 35-36
  - Eskom-dominated generation and transmission opening to Independent Power Producers through the REIPPPP bid windows, municipal distribution grids, and a very large SMME layer of solar and inverter installers filling the grid-decentralisation gap.
  - Also known as: Energy, Power, Electricity, Renewables
  - **Generation & Independent Power Producers** `ENR-GEN`
    - IPP projects financed under REIPPPP bid windows, embedded generation for mines and industry, and thermal fleet operators.
    - **REIPPPP / Bid Window IPPs** `ENR-GEN-IPP`
      - Project companies (SPVs) with 20-year PPAs, community and B-BBEE shareholding structures.
      - **Solar PV IPP Project Company** `ENR-GEN-IPP-SOLAR`
        - Single-asset utility-scale solar project companies. Headcount is tiny while turnover and asset value are large, so the scale band must be driven by turnover rather than employees.
      - **Wind IPP Project Company** `ENR-GEN-IPP-WIND`
      - **Battery Energy Storage IPP** `ENR-GEN-IPP-BESS`
      - **Project SPV & Shareholder Structuring** `ENR-GEN-IPP-SPV`
        - Deal structuring, community trusts and B-BBEE equity participation around a generation asset.
    - **Thermal Generation (Coal & Gas)** `ENR-GEN-THERM`
    - **Biomass, Biogas, Landfill Gas & Cogeneration** `ENR-GEN-COGEN`
    - **Embedded & Private Generation** `ENR-GEN-EMBED`
      - Behind-the-meter generation for mines, malls and industrial parks, and wheeling arrangements.
  - **Transmission, Distribution & Grid** `ENR-TRAN`
    - National transmission, municipal distribution networks and the grid-connection bottleneck that shapes every renewable project.
    - **Transmission Network Operations** `ENR-TRAN-GRID`
    - **Municipal Distribution Networks** `ENR-TRAN-DIST`
      - Metro and municipality electricity distribution, prepaid metering revenue and non-technical losses.
    - **Grid Connection & Substation Works** `ENR-TRAN-CONN`
  - **Solar PV & Storage** `ENR-SOLAR` _(informal economy)_
    - From utility-scale EPC down to single-phase residential installers - the fastest-growing SMME cohort in the country after the 2022-2024 load-shedding boom.
    - **Utility-Scale Solar EPC** `ENR-SOLAR-EPC`
    - **Commercial & Industrial Rooftop Solar** `ENR-SOLAR-CI`
      - **Wheeling & Corporate PPA Structuring** `ENR-SOLAR-CI-WHEEL`
      - **C&I Operations & Maintenance Contracts** `ENR-SOLAR-CI-MAINT`
      - **C&I Rooftop EPC Contractor** `ENR-SOLAR-CI-EPC`
    - **Residential Solar & Inverter Installation** `ENR-SOLAR-RES` _(informal economy)_
      - The grid-decentralisation gap: thousands of micro installers, many unregistered or newly formalised, working to SANS 10142-2 and NRS 097-2-1.
      - **Compliant Residential PV Installation** `ENR-SOLAR-RES-INSTALL`
        - Wireman's licence and CoC-issuing installers with municipal notification of the installation.
      - **Unregistered / Informal Inverter Installers** `ENR-SOLAR-RES-INFORMAL` _(informal economy)_
        - Tracked explicitly: a large share of installations are done without CoC or municipal sign-off, which is a data-quality and safety flag rather than an exclusion.
      - **Residential Solar Maintenance & Repair** `ENR-SOLAR-RES-MAINT` _(informal economy)_
    - **Battery Storage Integration** `ENR-SOLAR-BESS`
    - **Solar Operations, Maintenance & Cleaning** `ENR-SOLAR-OM`
    - **Solar Component Manufacturing & Assembly** `ENR-SOLAR-MFG`
  - **Wind** `ENR-WIND`
    - Eastern Cape and Western Cape wind corridors, tower and blade localisation content debates, and O&M service firms.
    - **Wind Farm Development & Operation** `ENR-WIND-FARM`
    - **Turbine Operations & Maintenance** `ENR-WIND-OM`
    - **Tower, Blade & Component Manufacturing** `ENR-WIND-MFG`
  - **Green Hydrogen & Derivatives** `ENR-HYDRO`
    - Boegoebaai and Saldanha green hydrogen and ammonia export projects, electrolyser supply chains and early refuelling infrastructure.
    - **Green Hydrogen & Ammonia Production** `ENR-HYDRO-PROD`
      - **Green Ammonia Export Project** `ENR-HYDRO-PROD-EXPORT`
        - Boegoebaai and Saldanha-type projects, typically large SPVs with consortium shareholding.
    - **Electrolyser Supply & Integration** `ENR-HYDRO-EQ`
    - **Hydrogen Derivatives Export & Terminals** `ENR-HYDRO-EXP`
  - **Petroleum, Fuels & Gas Downstream** `ENR-OIL`
    - Refining capacity rationalisation (Secunda, eThekwini), fuel wholesale and forecourt retail, LPG distribution and the NERSA-licensed petroleum supply chain.
    - **Refining & Petrochemical Conversion** `ENR-OIL-REF`
    - **Fuel Wholesale & Forecourt Retail** `ENR-OIL-FUEL`
      - Site-licensed forecourt operators, many franchise or dealer-owned; cross-referenced to RTL-FUEL.
    - **LPG & Gas Distribution** `ENR-OIL-LPG`
    - **Pipelines, Terminals & Storage** `ENR-OIL-PIPE`
    - **Lubricants & Bitumen** `ENR-OIL-LUBE`
  - **Energy Services, Installation & ESCO** `ENR-SVC` _(informal economy)_
    - Electrical contracting, performance contracting, metering and energy audits - the SMME backbone of grid decentralisation.
    - **Electrical Contracting & CoC Issuing** `ENR-SVC-ELEC`
    - **Energy Service Companies & Performance Contracting** `ENR-SVC-ESC`
    - **Smart Metering, Sub-Metering & AMI** `ENR-SVC-METER`
    - **Energy Audits & Measurement and Verification** `ENR-SVC-AUDIT`
    - **Generators & Backup Power Supply** `ENR-SVC-GENSET` _(informal economy)_
  - **Energy Efficiency & Carbon Management** `ENR-EFF`
    - **Thermal Efficiency & Insulation** `ENR-EFF-THERM`
    - **Motor, Drive & Compressed Air Efficiency** `ENR-EFF-MOTOR`
    - **Carbon Tax, Offsets & MRV Reporting** `ENR-EFF-CARBON`
- **Agriculture, Forestry & Agribusiness** `AGR` — SIC 01-03
  - A barbell structure: large commercial co-operatives and export-led horticulture on one side, emerging and smallholder farmers on the other. Regional logistics hubs (Sundays River citrus, Stellenbosch wine, Bothaville grain, Tzaneen subtropics) determine where the registered entities cluster.
  - Also known as: Agriculture, Agribusiness, Farming, Agri
  - **Field Crops** `AGR-FIELD`
    - Maize triangle (Free State, North West, Mpumalanga), wheat in the Swartland and Free State, soy and sunflower expansion, KZN and Mpumalanga sugarcane.
    - **White & Yellow Maize Production** `AGR-FIELD-MAIZE`
      - **Commercial Maize Production** `AGR-FIELD-MAIZE-COMM`
        - Free State, North West and Mpumalanga maize triangle producers delivering to co-operative silos.
      - **Emerging & Smallholder Maize Production** `AGR-FIELD-MAIZE-EMERG` _(informal economy)_
    - **Wheat Production** `AGR-FIELD-WHEAT`
    - **Soybean & Sunflower Production** `AGR-FIELD-SOY`
    - **Sugarcane Production** `AGR-FIELD-SUGAR`
      - KZN North Coast and Mpumalanga cane growers and mill supply areas.
    - **Potatoes & Field Vegetables** `AGR-FIELD-POT`
  - **Horticulture & Export Fruit** `AGR-HORT`
    - Citrus Growers' Association export volumes, deciduous fruit in the Western Cape, subtropical expansion in Limpopo and Mpumalanga.
    - **Citrus Production & Export** `AGR-HORT-CITRUS`
      - Sundays River Valley, Limpopo and Senwes-belt citrus, packed for EU and Asian markets.
      - **Citrus Packhouse & Export Preparation** `AGR-HORT-CITRUS-PACK`
        - The regional logistics hub node: packhouses feeding Port Elizabeth, Cape Town and Durban cold chains.
      - **Emerging Citrus Growers** `AGR-HORT-CITRUS-EMERG` _(informal economy)_
    - **Deciduous Fruit (Pome & Stone)** `AGR-HORT-DECID`
    - **Subtropical Fruit (Avocado, Macadamia, Mango)** `AGR-HORT-SUB`
    - **Fresh Vegetables & Tunnel Farming** `AGR-HORT-VEG` _(informal economy)_
    - **Packhouse, Sorting & Export Preparation** `AGR-HORT-PACK`
    - **Export Marketing & Cold Chain Logistics** `AGR-HORT-EXPORT`
      - Cross-referenced to WHL-COLD and TRA-COLD; port scheduling at Port Elizabeth, Cape Town and Durban.
  - **Wine & Table Grapes** `AGR-VITI`
    - Cape winelands estates, co-operatives and cellars, plus the Northern Cape table grape industry around Upington.
    - **Wine Production & Cellars** `AGR-VITI-WINE`
      - **Estate Wine Production & Cellar Operations** `AGR-VITI-WINE-ESTATE`
      - **Wine Co-operative Cellars** `AGR-VITI-WINE-COOP`
    - **Table Grape Production** `AGR-VITI-TABLE`
    - **Bulk Wine & Private Label Supply** `AGR-VITI-BULK`
    - **Brandy, Spirits & Distillation** `AGR-VITI-BRANDY`
  - **Livestock & Animal Production** `AGR-LIVE`
    - Beef and sheep farming across the interior, KZN and Mpumalanga feedlots, and the communal grazing economy.
    - **Cattle & Beef Production** `AGR-LIVE-BEEF`
    - **Sheep, Wool & Mohair** `AGR-LIVE-SHEEP`
    - **Feedlots & Weaner Trading** `AGR-LIVE-FEEDLOT`
    - **Abattoirs & Red Meat Processing** `AGR-LIVE-ABAT`
    - **Communal Livestock & Grazing** `AGR-LIVE-COMM` _(informal economy)_
  - **Dairy** `AGR-DAIRY`
    - Southern Cape, KZN Midlands and Gauteng dairy belts, with a concentrated processor layer.
    - **Dairy Farming & Milk Production** `AGR-DAIRY-FARM`
    - **Milk Collection & Cooling Depots** `AGR-DAIRY-COOL`
  - **Poultry & Protein** `AGR-POULTRY`
    - Vertically integrated broiler producers plus a long tail of independent broiler houses and day-old chick suppliers.
    - **Broiler Production & Grow-Out Houses** `AGR-POULTRY-BROILER`

    - **Agricultural Co-operatives** `AGR-COMM-COOP`

      - **Full-Service Agricultural Co-operative** `AGR-COMM-COOP-FULL`

    - **Sawmilling & Board Manufacturing** `AGR-FOREST-SAW`
    - **Community Forestry & Small-Grower Schemes** `AGR-FOREST-COMM`
  - **Fisheries & Aquaculture** `AGR-FISH`

- **Manufacturing & Industrial Processing** `MFG` — SIC 10-33
  - Automotive assembly clusters in the Eastern Cape and Gauteng, heavy chemicals and fuel synthesis at Secunda, steel and ferroalloys, and FMCG manufacturing that feeds the dominant retail chains.
  - Also known as: Manufacturing, Industrial, Processing
  - **Automotive & Component Manufacturing** `MFG-AUTO`
    - OEM assembly plants (Volkswagen and Isuzu in Gqeberha, BMW in East London, Ford in Silverton, Mercedes-Benz in East London) with Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier parks around them, supported by APDP and then AIS incentive programmes.
    - Also known as: Automotive, Motor industry, OEM
    - **Vehicle Assembly (OEM)** `MFG-AUTO-OEM`
      - Complete vehicle assembly plants, largely export-led into European and UK markets.
      - **Export-Oriented Vehicle Assembly Plant** `MFG-AUTO-OEM-EXPORT`
        - Eastern Cape and Gauteng assembly plants producing predominantly for European and UK export markets.
    - **Tier 1 Component Manufacturing** `MFG-AUTO-CAT1`
      - Direct-to-OEM suppliers, frequently foreign-owned subsidiaries with local B-BBEE participation.
      - **Exhaust Systems & Catalytic Converters** `MFG-AUTO-CAT1-EXH`

      - **Seating, Interiors & Trim** `MFG-AUTO-CAT1-INT`
      - **Wiring Harnesses & Electrical Assemblies** `MFG-AUTO-CAT1-WIRE`
      - **Tyre & Rubber Component Manufacturing** `MFG-AUTO-CAT1-TYRE`
      - **Castings, Forgings & Machined Engine Parts** `MFG-AUTO-CAT1-CAST`
    - **Tier 2 & Sub-Assembly Manufacturing** `MFG-AUTO-CAT2` _(informal economy)_
      - Where most black-owned and SMME automotive participation sits, typically supplying Tier 1 plants.
    - **Aftermarket Parts & Accessories** `MFG-AUTO-AFTER`
    - **Electric Vehicle & Battery Components** `MFG-AUTO-EV`
    - **Tooling, Dies, Jigs & Press Tools** `MFG-AUTO-TOOL`
