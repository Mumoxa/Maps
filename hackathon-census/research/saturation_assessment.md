**Statement for the record:** Web research cannot mathematically prove that every historical participant has been found. This database should be cited as **"Best achievable public-source coverage as of 2026-08-26 (Phase 1 of an ongoing census — not saturated)"** — never as complete. In particular: the absence of Mpumalanga and Northern Cape *venues* in the current master table is a research gap, not evidence that no hackathons occurred there (Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence).
