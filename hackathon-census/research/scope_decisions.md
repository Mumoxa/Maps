# Scope Decisions (inclusion/exclusion register)

## Included (with scope notes where borderline)
- Entelect Challenge — months-long national AI coding competition; "coding challenge / code jam" scope; flagged in event notes.
- DIRISA Student Datathons — datathons (explicitly in scope).
- ITWeb Security Summit Hackathon series (#SS19Hack 2019, #SS22Hack, #SS23Hack, #SS24Hack, #SSHACK26, + Northern Cape edition #SS25HACK 2025) — cybersecurity hackathons with CTF components (Phase 2 discovery; organiser = Geekulcha + ITWeb).
- MICT SETA National Skills Challenge Hackathon — SETA skills hackathon regionals + finals.
- G20 Tourism Hackathon 2025 — Department of Tourism national govtech-style hackathon (G20 SA presidency programme).
- SA Space Design Competition national finals — engineering design sprint at UCT; international outcome recorded as notes only.
- HackAtom SA national round — energy/engineering competition, national sprint round.
- UmojaHack Africa (Zindi) — pan-African online datathon; SA relevance documented (Johannesburg-datelined organiser press release, SA sponsors, SA-winning universities); individual names unpublished.
- HackCorruption South Africa 2022 — regional hybrid event; SA relevance via Johannesburg winners' boot camp; winners' nationalities deliberately not inferred.
- Wits Minecraft Education Challenge 2018 — build/design challenge (game-jam adjacent).
- Wits supercomputing team awards 2018 — computing competition awards; context thin, flagged for re-verification.
- Kuunda Disrupt schools STEAM competition 2024; IEB-TechWays National AI Hackathon 2026 — youth/schools challenge scope.
- IATF 2025 Healthcare Hackathon — physically outside SA; included only under the online/hybrid SA-connection rule.
- Employee/internal hackathons (Kuunda Disrupt, Absa Technology Hackathon, Vodacom Hack-alympics) — event level only; winners rarely public.
- Tech4Africa Hackathon 2015 — hackathon track within 4-day conference (completeness E pending winners).

## Reviewed and excluded (documented so the boundary is auditable)
- Microsoft Imagine Cup Junior 2024 (Brescia House "Rainbow Soldiers") — submission-based global AI competition without sprint format.
- GSMA Open Gateway Africa Ignite Hackathon 2026 — no verified SA connection/venue for the named winners.
- GameUp Africa Jam for Democracy 2024 — winners announced in Lagos; no SA connection evidenced (Phase 2).
- AppsAfrica Awards, Inspiring Fifty SA, MTN/FNB "App of the Year"-style app competitions — awards/app competitions, not sprint challenges.
- Vodacom consumer "competition winners" page — promotional draws.
- Technovation 2019 (SA participation) — global girls' tech programme; not a hackathon format.
- SANS Holiday Hack Challenge, Smart India Hackathon, Positive Hack Days — non-SA events surfaced by search noise.
- Judges, mentors, organisers excluded from contestant records (SS-series judges incl. Nithen Naidoo, Ivan Regasek; GirlCode CEO; Absa design directors). One organiser-turned-participant case noted on its own evidence (Tiyani Nghonyama, Hack4Water 2016 participant page).
