## Inclusion standards

A company was added only where an official company page or an identified development-owner page explicitly supported its involvement in **property development, property investing, construction/development management, or related built-environment activities**. Site claims are first-party representations, not independent financial audits. Direct source links and checked date are retained per industry and location attribute.

A company selling property as an estate agency **alone** is not a developer and was excluded from this batch; likewise an estate project is not automatically treated as a company. All incoming names were compared with the existing canonical register before insertion. Older curated data and company IDs were left intact.

An entry carrying `status: verified` means the published page backs the limited development-activity claim. It **does not establish official SMME classification or statutory size**. No employee numbers, revenue, CIPC registration status, building valuations, ownership percentage or production capacity were guessed. Organisations that are described as boutique, independent or family-owned by their website may still be larger than a statutory small enterprise.

This batch spans residential estates and apartments; retail, offices, warehouses, project-development and refurbishment operations; small developer-builders; and family-owned investing groups. These are **different operating models**, exposed via the separate industry links. Firms offering property development consultancy only are included where the page explicitly describes development activity; they must not be automatically treated as the developer/asset owner of a named project.

Two organisations were named as developers by development-project websites: **Sign Power Investments** via Intaba Ridge and **Crown Homes** via Capital Hill. These are source-backed developer identifications but should undergo legal-entity and website confirmation before detailed corporate claims are added.

## Evidence and quality boundaries

- Verified *activity* is not verified *ownership*; company affiliations were not converted into shareholding trees.
- Primary industry is only a sorting convenience, never proof of turnover mix.
- The listed location is an explicitly named office or operation; it does not imply the company has no other sites.
- Source links lead to pages used for the inclusion claim; if a website changes, re-verification is required.
- Named subsidiary or family group structure is described as contextual text where sourced; separate legal entities require their own canonical identity and corporate evidence before linking.
- Footprint remains "not established" until sufficiently comparable, sourced metrics are available. Do not classify these companies as statutory SMMEs based on their market visibility.
- The 81-name historical property-workspace intake was reconciled. Its Feenstra Group record now points to the canonical company; the remaining unmatched names continue in an explicitly unverified research queue.

## Suggested next research

1. Extend beyond easily searchable company-owned websites: registered developers, municipal approvals, residential developments, industry bodies, contractor/developer partnerships and regional commercial parks.
2. Validate legal identity, geographic footprints, headcount, operational scale and project counts with source attribution and entity scope.
3. Build a future standalone `development-project` data type for projects, developer/contractor roles, project stage, actual geography, partners, and cited budgets; project names alone are not companies.
4. Re-run source/date review periodically. This record is an import snapshot, not a live claim that all websites remain unchanged.

## Validation protocol

Use `npm run validate:organizations`, `npm run validate:taxonomy`, `npm test`, `npm run build`, `npm run smoke:routes`, and `npm run audit:ui` in CI.
