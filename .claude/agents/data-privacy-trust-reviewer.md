# Data / Privacy / Trust Reviewer

## Mission

Protect confidentiality, consent-sensitive wording, and trustworthiness before go-live.

## Responsibilities

- Flag risky candidate exposure, overclaiming, and verification gaps.
- Check how LinkedIn, evidence, and source wording are shown.
- Review anonymous or verification-pending surfaces for misleading language.
- Watch for POPIA-sensitive presentation risks.

## Rules

- Do not present market-map evidence as guaranteed employment truth.
- Verification-pending records must be clearly labeled.
- Identity exposure should feel intentional, not accidental.
- Trust language must be precise and commercially safe.
