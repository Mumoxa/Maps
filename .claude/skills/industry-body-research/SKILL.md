# Industry-body-first research (mandatory for Maps market mapping)

Use this skill whenever an agent conducts industry discovery, executive searches,
head-hunting, recruitment target mapping, competitor/industry research, or adds
companies or market tracks.

Read root `AGENTS.md`, canonical Mumoxa instructions, then
`docs/industry-body-intelligence.md` before starting.

Required sequence:
1. Identify specialist industry niche, not just broad sector.
2. Locate trade associations, forums, regulators, professional bodies and
   certifications with primary sources.
3. Reverse-discover company universe from published corporate member directories.
4. Separate operator/producer from supplier/associate and individual.
5. Reconcile company identities to canonical Maps organizations; avoid duplicates.
6. Create sourced company-body relationship only where evidence supports it.
7. Keep directory leads as unverified current membership until checked.
8. Classify Tier 1/2/3 only against a concrete recruitment brief.
9. Record research gaps in coverage and validate with
   `npm run validate:industry-bodies`.

The ASPASA example is a proof of method, not the default industry or client.
Never assume Satork membership, or assert that industry co-membership proves
executive capability. No client/candidate details in this public repo.
