## Evidence discipline

- I used only public web evidence: LinkedIn public snippets/pages, SAP/customer/provider pages, public job adverts, and provider reference pages.
- I did **not** include emails or phone numbers, even where public people databases exposed them.
- If a profile title came from a public people directory rather than direct LinkedIn text, I marked the evidence as weaker and added a caveat.
- If SAP CAR/POSDTA appeared only in “people also viewed,” a liked post, or an unrelated search-result artefact, I excluded it or put it in a caution tab.
